# Korišteni prompt set

Na svakoj je slici korištena postojeća pozadina kao edit target, uz zajedničku uputu:

> Preserve the original portrait scene, elevated camera angle, character and raft positions, shoreline, and open water on the upper-right. Keep exactly two young adult men: a strong slightly chubby man resting on the beach and staring blankly at the sea, and a skinny nerdy repairer with clearly visible round glasses, rope in one hand and a small hammer in the other. Keep the handmade raft, crate, loose rope, palms, sand, rocks and water. Preserve calm negative space on the right for UI. No text, interface, logos, watermark, extra people, missing glasses, missing rope or missing hammer.

Stilski dodatak za svaku varijantu:

1. **Flat graphic:** clean flat indie-game illustration, bold simplified geometric shapes, limited tropical palette, crisp silhouettes, minimal texture and restrained shading; no anime, photorealism, 3D or glossy rendering.
2. **Watercolor and ink:** traditional watercolor-and-ink adventure-book illustration, translucent washes, cold-press paper grain, loose expressive contours, soft color blooms and gentle sunlight; no anime, 3D or hard digital cel shading.
3. **Retro comic:** retro European adventure comic and mid-century travel print, uniform ink contours, muted print colors, halftone texture, angular shadows and weathered paper; no anime, superhero anatomy, speech bubbles or 3D.
4. **Low-poly 3D:** charming low-poly game diorama, faceted geometry, chunky forms, hand-painted low-resolution textures, soft ambient occlusion and translucent turquoise water; no anime, realistic skin or glossy AAA rendering.
5. **Pixel art:** high-quality 32-bit pixel art, visible square pixel grid, crisp hard-edged clusters, selective dithering, sprite-like characters and no antialiasing; no smooth vector edges, painterly brushwork or 3D.
6. **Clay stop-motion:** handcrafted stop-motion miniature made from clay, carved wood, cotton rope, painted resin water and real sand, with fingerprints and handmade imperfections; no anime, flat illustration, glossy plastic toys or extreme blur.
