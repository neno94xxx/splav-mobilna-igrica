Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Raft Escape, provided by Nedjeljko Miloslavic (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”), handles information when you use the game.

Information stored on your device

Raft Escape does not require an account and does not ask you to submit your name, email address, or other contact details. Game progress—such as resources, upgrades, settings, completed rides, and advertising frequency—is stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this local save data.

Advertising and information processed by Google

Raft Escape uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display interstitial advertisements. Google may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • app interactions, including app launches, taps, and ad views; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google may use this information to provide and measure advertising, perform analytics, prevent fraud, and maintain the security of its services. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Your privacy choices

Where required by law, Raft Escape uses Google’s consent tools to present advertising privacy choices. You can accept, refuse, or manage the available options in the consent message. You can revisit those choices from the privacy options provided in the game when that option is legally required and available.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. Information described above may be processed by Google as our advertising service provider under Google’s terms and privacy policies. We do not operate a separate analytics service or a server that receives your game-save data.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset the game, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall Raft Escape. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google’s applicable policies and your privacy choices. You may contact us with a privacy or deletion question using the address below.

Children’s privacy

Raft Escape is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly ask children—or any other users—to submit personal contact information. Advertising is handled in accordance with the app’s audience configuration, applicable law, and Google’s policies.

Security

Raft Escape minimizes the information it handles directly by keeping game progress on your device. Network data handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
